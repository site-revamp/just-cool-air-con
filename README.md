# Just Cool Aircon Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

Just Cool Aircon Engineering Pte. Ltd. provides aircon servicing, installation, maintenance, and troubleshooting for homes, offices, and commercial properties in Singapore. The proposed homepage helps visitors identify the right service, assess the company’s experience, and make an enquiry.

## Main Improvements

1. **Make services easier to find.** The original homepage gives visitors limited guidance from the introduction to service selection. A service-focused hero and six consistent service cards with direct enquiry links would make the options easier to scan and act on.
2. **Make company credentials more visible.** The company’s published experience and project figures are easy to miss. A dedicated section would highlight technician experience and workmanship, with genuine testimonials or verified reviews where available.
3. **Shorten the mobile enquiry path.** The existing form and contact options could be easier to reach. Prominent enquiry buttons and a persistent mobile bar with Call and WhatsApp actions would give visitors a direct path to contact the company. The WhatsApp destination needs confirmation before publication.

## Design Improvements

Clearer headings, typography, and spacing would distinguish the hero, service cards, and credibility section. Consistent enquiry labels and visible operating hours would help visitors identify the next step.
